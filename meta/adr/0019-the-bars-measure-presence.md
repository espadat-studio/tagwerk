# Every bar and the day cap measure presence

ADR-0011 settled that the day cap is a presence threshold, and implemented it as "every credited minute" because at the time the two were the same number: each present minute was credited exactly once. ADR-0018 broke that identity and did not follow the consequence through. `bar_line` kept reddening on the credited sum, so the burnout check inherited the invoice's arithmetic. Monday 2026-09-21 rendered `18:40` in `tagwerk week` against 11:21 actually spent at the machine, and because the credited total is never below presence, the day cap now trips early rather than late: an 8-hour threshold fires at roughly four real hours on a day spent in two kinds. A burnout signal that overstates a day by seven hours and nineteen minutes is worse than none, because it is read and believed.

Presence is counted in the same walk that credits the minutes, and `attribute` returns both. A minute counts once toward presence when a span of any kind but `off` covers it, or when the sensors put the user at the machine. The span clause is not a special case: `CONTEXT.md` already defines a span as overriding the sensors for its whole range, and the idle and poll events are the sensors that decide presence, so a non-off span asserting time necessarily asserts presence with it. Without the clause the 202 imported timew spans, and every `tagwerk fix`, would read `0:00` to the burnout check.

Every bar now runs to presence: the day rows in `week`, the ISO-week rows in `month`, the label that reddens and the number at the end. The buckets survive as the proportions inside the bar, which is what they were always drawn for, so the mix of a day stays visible while its length stops lying. The one paid threshold left is the `week` footer, exactly as ADR-0011 placed it. The rule is now sayable in one line: bars and the day cap measure presence, the `week` footer measures paid, and the tables carry both a credited `total` and a `present` row so the invoice number and the burnout number sit side by side and can be told apart.

Rejected: leaving the bar drawn to credited minutes and printing presence beside it, which keeps the misleading figure first and makes every row two numbers wide. Rejected: swapping only the trailing number, the smallest diff, which leaves a bar whose length contradicts the number printed at its end -- the defect this decision exists to remove. Rejected: defining presence as sensor evidence alone, the literal reading of "in front of the laptop", which is the same number on a sensor-only day and silently drops every manually entered hour on any other.

Consequences: `over_cap` in `day --json` is computed against presence, so the Omarchy bar widget inherits the corrected rule without a second threshold in QML, as ADR-0011 intended. That widget still draws its fill from `total_minutes` and must move to `present_minutes`, or its length and its colour will answer different questions. `render_table` gained a `present` row, so every caller reading the last line of a table for the total now reads one line up. The `week` footer is untouched.
